Who we are

Inspyx is made by INSPYX LLC, Miami Gardens, Florida. Questions about privacy: support@inspyx.com or (888) 772-2919.

Who this covers

  • Home inspectors who use the Inspyx app or our website.
  • The people in their inspections (clients, real estate agents and others), whose details an inspector enters or a client enters on an inspector's booking page.
  • Clients who sign an agreement, pay or open a report through Inspyx, and people who call an inspector's Mia line.
  • Visitors to inspyx.com and callers to our own phone line.

The inspector decides what to record about their clients and agents, and how to use it. Inspyx handles that information for the inspector, to provide the service. Inspectors own their client relationships, and we never sell anyone's data.

What we collect, and why

  1. Your account. Your name, email address and sign-in, handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they tell us your name and email address. We never see your password, and Face ID stays on your iPhone. If you sign in with Apple, we keep the token Apple gives us, only so that deleting your account also ends Inspyx's access at Apple. We note the day you agreed to our Terms and this policy, and how you first signed in. You can add business details (your license number, logo, photo and signature), which print on your reports. Your company details, picture, logo, signature, agreements, templates and price list are kept on Inspyx's servers.
  2. Your inspections. Property addresses and details; your clients' and agents' names, phone numbers and emails; findings, notes, answers and photos (photos can show the inside of a home); your client's signature on your agreement; and your fee for the inspection, whether it's paid, and payments you record. These are saved on your iPhone and kept on our servers too, so you can see and finish them at inspyx.com, or on another iPhone or iPad signed in to your account. Your iPhone also keeps a private copy of all of it, photo and video files included, in your account's own folder in our file store (at Supabase), so a lost or replaced iPhone never means a lost inspection: sign in on the new one and bring them back. The same folder brings an inspection's photo and video files to your other iPhone or iPad, so both show the same inspection. Nothing in it goes to anyone else's account or device. Copies go over Wi-Fi, or over mobile data only if you allow it. Photo and video files otherwise leave your iPhone only when you share or export them, or when a feature below sends what it needs to do its job.
  3. Your inspections on the website. So you can see your inspections, calendar and the people on them on inspyx.com, their details are kept on Inspyx's servers with your account.
  4. People you keep. The clients and agents you keep in People (their names, phone numbers, emails, companies and your notes about them) are kept on Inspyx's servers with your account, so they show on the website as well as in the app, and they're removed when you delete your account.
  5. Inspections from the app you used before. When you bring in your past inspections, we keep each one's date, address, client and the client's email and phone, agents, fee, services, inspector, order number and notes, and where it came from, on Inspyx's servers with your account, so they show on the website as well as in the app. They're removed when you delete your account.
  6. Bringing your data in. When you bring in a file of your clients, agents or past inspections on the website, your browser reads it there: the file itself is never sent to us, only the people and inspections you choose to keep. To bring them in straight from Spectora, you paste your own Spectora API key. In the app, your iPhone asks Spectora directly. On the website, our server asks Spectora for each page and passes it to your browser without keeping it. Your key is used only for those requests: we never keep it or write it down. So no one can use our website to flood Spectora, our server counts how many requests your account makes each hour, never what they carry, and the count is removed when you delete your account.
  7. Your booking page. If you turn on your booking page, your clients can book you at inspyx.com/book/your-name. They see your name, company, services, prices and open times. We work out your open times from the hours you set and your inspections' times only (when each starts and ends, never the address or the client). When a client books, we keep what they enter (their name, email, phone, the property's address and details, and the services they pick) and send it to your app. Booking emails (and Inspyx's other automatic emails) go out through Amazon's email service. We note whether each one was delivered, bounced or marked as spam, so you can see it reached them. If they stop partway, we keep what they entered for 30 days so you can follow up.
  8. Agreements signed online. When a client books, we email them your agreement to sign on their phone or computer. We keep the exact text they were sent, their initials and drawn signature, their typed name, when they signed, their device's browser name, and a scrambled form of their internet address, so the signature can be shown to be theirs. The signed copy (a PDF) is emailed to them and to you, and kept on our servers while your account is open. We remind a client to sign only while they haven't, at most three times, and never after the inspection starts.
  9. Reports you send. When you send a report, your client gets a private link to it at inspyx.com. We keep the report there while your account is open, and note when the link is first and last opened, so you can see your client has it. On their report link, your client or their agent can list the repairs or credits they want for its findings. We keep their list with the report, and any copy they choose to share at inspyx.com/repair. They send it themselves; Inspyx sends nothing.
  10. Card payments from your clients. If you take card payments, your client pays on Stripe's page, into your own Stripe account. Stripe receives their card; we never see it. We keep what was paid, when, and for which inspection, so your app and the website can show it.
  11. Texts to your clients. Only to a client who agreed to texts, and only about their own inspection. Texts go out through Amazon's text service, from Inspyx's number on your behalf. We keep each text and when it was sent.
  12. Mia, your phone line. If you turn on Mia for your business, calls to your Mia number are answered by Mia through Retell, our phone service, which turns the call into words so Mia can answer. We keep each call's time and length (for your minutes) and the message Mia takes: the caller's name and number, why they called, and any address, services or times they mention. Mia sends that message to you.
  13. Emails you forward. If you forward a client's email to your Inspyx address (your-name@book.inspyx.com), we keep its sender, subject and words for 90 days so Spex can draft your reply. Attachments aren't kept. Amazon holds the email itself only until we've kept its words, and never longer than 7 days. Spex only drafts: nothing is sent until you tap Send.
  14. Spex, the voice companion. While Spex is on, what you say, and any photo you show Spex, goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, so Spex can understand and answer. Our server keeps a usage record for each session (your account, how long it ran, and what it cost us), not the audio or the words. The captions stay on your phone. Spex listens only while it's on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  15. Spex Chat and Spex's writing help. When you type to Spex, or ask it to rewrite a finding (in the app or on the website), an email or a text, check a label's details, or convert a template, what you send goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, which writes the answer. Nothing changes until you keep it. Your chats stay on your iPhone. Our server keeps a usage record (your account, and what it cost us), not your words.
  16. Property lookups. When you choose an address, our server sends it to public-record, weather and map services to fill in the inspection: RentCast (property facts), the U.S. Census Bureau's address service, the National Weather Service, NOAA and the Aviation Weather Center (the weather when you start), and Google (Street View and the view from above). Address suggestions while you type come from Apple Maps, through your iPhone.
  17. Crash reports. If the app breaks, a report of where and why goes to Inspyx's own server, hosted by Supabase. We leave out your name, and remove email addresses and phone numbers. We keep each report for up to 90 days. You can turn this off: ☰ → Settings → Send crash reports.
  18. Your plan. Billing runs through Stripe. Stripe receives your email address and card; we never see or store card numbers. We keep your plan's status, dates and price.
  19. Your numbers. Inspyx works out how long each inspection took (time on site, time to the report) on your iPhone. If you tick "Be a founding inspector" when you sign up, your app sends Inspyx these numbers for each inspection you finish in your first month: when it started, when you were last on site, when the report was made and shared, and how many findings (and how many Spex added), photos and items it has. Never a client's name, an address, or anything your report says. After your first month, nothing more is sent. If you don't tick the box, nothing leaves your phone unless you export it.
  20. Try Spex on our website. If you try Spex on inspyx.com, what you say goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, so Spex can answer. We keep no recording and no words. To keep the demo fair, our server keeps a scrambled form of your internet address for the day.
  21. Our website and phone line. If you apply for the pilot on our website (the form is a Google form) or call our phone line, we keep your name, contact details and what you tell us, so we can answer you. If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email address, the two answers you tap, and which page and which ad or link brought you, so we can write to you when the pilot opens. Our phone line is answered by Mia, an AI receptionist, through a service called Retell. Our website uses no cookies and no ad trackers.

How we use it

  • To run Inspyx for you: your inspections, reports, bookings, agreements, payments, texts, emails, Mia and Spex.
  • To help you when you ask us, and to keep accounts safe and stop abuse.
  • To make Inspyx better: from crash reports, and from patterns across many inspectors, never by reading one inspector's records for their own sake.
  • To tell you about Inspyx: emails about your account and plan, which you always get, and news about Inspyx, which you can stop with the link in any of them.
  • To show what Inspyx does: we may publish totals and averages from founders' numbers (for example, "founders finished their reports 40% faster"), never naming you or your clients.
  • Testimonials: we use your name, company, picture or words in our marketing only if you say yes, and we stop if you change your mind.

What we never do

  • Sell anyone's data.
  • Use your clients' details to market to them.
  • Use your inspection data to advertise.
  • Let our AI provider train its models on what you send: Google handles it for us under terms that don't allow that.
  • Sell anyone's mobile number or their OK to receive texts, or share either with third parties for marketing. A client's number and their text consent are used only to send texts about their own inspection, from Inspyx's number on their inspector's behalf. When someone agrees to texts on a booking page, we keep the words they agreed to, when, and a scrambled form of their internet address, as proof of their consent. Replying STOP ends the texts.

Who we share with

  • Our service providers, each only for its part: Supabase (our database, file store and sign-in), Google Cloud (Spex and Gemini, Street View and the view from above), Amazon Web Services (emails and texts), Stripe (your plan, and your clients' card payments), Retell (Mia's calls), Vercel (our website), Apple (sign-in with Apple and address suggestions), RentCast and the public address and weather services named above, and Google Forms (the pilot form).
  • People you choose to send things to, such as a report you share.
  • Real estate agents you email a report to. When you email a report to someone as their agent, Inspyx gives that agent a private link to one page listing the reports you have emailed to that same address: each property's address and date, and the report. It shows only what you already sent them, and you can turn it off at any time. To stop anyone guessing an agent page's private link, our server keeps a scrambled form of the visitor's internet address for up to a day, then deletes it.
  • A buyer of the business: if INSPYX LLC is ever merged or sold, your information moves with it, and this policy still applies to it.
  • Anyone the law requires us to.

Where it's kept

Inspyx's servers, and our providers' for the parts they handle, are in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it, delete your account, or delete the app.
  • On our servers: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your account and your records from our servers right away. We keep only what the law or our accounts require: Stripe's billing records, a record of any refund, and Spex usage records. If Apple won't yet take back a sign-in token, we keep that token, naming no account, only until Apple does. If you get your money back, we keep a scrambled form of your email address and card even after your account is deleted, only so the same person can't get a second refund.
  • Booking pages: a booking someone started but didn't finish, 30 days; finished bookings and signed agreements, while your account is open; forwarded emails, 90 days. To stop anyone scraping a booking page, our server keeps a scrambled form of the visitor's internet address for up to a day.
  • Mia's calls and messages, your numbers, and reports you send: while your account is open. Totals and averages already worked out from founders' numbers name no one, so they stay.
  • Cloud copies of your iPhone: while your account is open. Deleting your account deletes them.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • Turn crash reports off: ☰ → Settings → Send crash reports.
  • Let cloud copies use mobile data, or keep them to Wi-Fi: ☰ → Settings → Use mobile data for photos and clips.
  • Turn Spex off at any time.
  • Export everything: ☰ → Export.
  • Delete your account in the app: ☰ → Account → Delete your account.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information: support@inspyx.com.
  • Stop Inspyx's news emails with the link in any of them.
  • Take back a yes to a testimonial: write to support@inspyx.com.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what information we hold about you, for a copy of it, to correct it, or to delete it, and you won't be treated differently for asking. Write to support@inspyx.com. We answer within 45 days, and we may need to confirm it's you first. We don't sell personal information, or share it for advertising that follows you around the web.

Security

  • Data on your iPhone is protected by the iPhone's own encryption.
  • Connections to our servers are encrypted.
  • Every change to an inspection is kept in a tamper-evident record, and a signed agreement keeps the exact text that was signed.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.

Children

Inspyx is for professionals, and it isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post changes on this page, and tell you in the app when a change matters.
