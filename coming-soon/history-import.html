Bring in past inspections

Your inspection history from the app you used before, from its export or straight from Spectora: Inspyx keeps every inspection on your list, here and on your phone, and brings the clients and agents it names into People. One you already have is never kept twice.

Your past inspections, read straight from your Spectora account with an API key: nothing to export.

Where to find your key

  1. Open Spectora’s developer site (it opens in a new tab) and sign in with your Spectora login.
  2. Choose Create API Key.
  3. Copy the key and paste it below.
  4. Inspyx uses it once, for this import, to read your past inspections. It isn’t kept, and nothing in Spectora changes.

What each column holds

Pick the column that holds the date, the address or the client: an inspection can’t be read without one.
