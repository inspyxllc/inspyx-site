Who we are

Inspyx is made by INSPYX LLC, a Florida company based in Miami Gardens, Florida.

The company was founded in 2026 by Moises Avila, a licensed Florida home inspector.

What we make

Inspyx is an iPhone app for home inspectors, with a voice companion.

  • Spex is the companion you talk to during an inspection. You say what you see, and Spex takes the photo, marks it and files the finding in the right room. Every finding goes straight into the report.
  • Mia is a voice receptionist. She answers the phone, answers the usual questions and books the job on your calendar.
  • Spex Business handles the requests that come in by email or through the form on your website.

Where we are today

Inspyx is getting ready for a pilot with its first 10 inspectors. Spex works today. Mia and Spex Business arrive with the pilot.

If you are a home inspector and would like to take part, you can join the waitlist.
