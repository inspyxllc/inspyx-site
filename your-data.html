What we stand for

Your clients are your clients. Full stop.

We never sell your data

We never sell inspector data. Not your client list, not your inspection history, not your numbers. Ever.

We never market to your clients

We never put third-party offers in front of your clients — in your portal, in your reports, anywhere.

Zero cut of your payments

Inspyx takes zero cut of your payments — no percentage, no per-transaction fee, no tech-fee remainder kept.

Your own account

When payments run through Inspyx, they run through your own merchant account — payouts straight to your bank, and the account and its history stay yours if you ever leave. Payments are optional forever: your own Stripe, Square, cash, or check costs you nothing here.

If you ever leave

Leaving is part of the deal, so it belongs on the page before you join. Your inspections, your reports, your templates and your client records are yours — they were never ours to keep. Your reports are documents you have already sent to your clients and agents, and they stay sent. And if your payments ever run through Inspyx, they run through your own merchant account: the account and its history stay yours if you leave, because they were always yours.

We would rather you stayed because the product earns it — not because your own records are hard to take with you.

What happened elsewhere
