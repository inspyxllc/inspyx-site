What happens next

1

You apply

You started with your email on the home page and finished the form above — six required fields, plus one optional question. Nothing else is asked of you at this stage.

2

We read it, and we call you

On the call we build your starting point together — your template brought over and your report time written down — so you start from something finished, not a blank account. We review every application personally. If you are a fit for the first group, we call you and talk it through — and we will tell you plainly if the timing is not right.

3

We move you in

You sign in to your current software yourself. We pull across everything your software lets us reach, then show you all of it — you review it and confirm before the move is final, and anything that couldn't move is listed for you there, never dropped silently. We revoke our access when the move is done. Your 30 free days start on a business that is already set up.

Applications are read in a weekly review. When your application is in, you receive a personal status link: it shows your real position in the founding cohort — how many inspectors are ahead of you and how many applied after you — from the same ledger as the public count, and it updates as places move.

The rules of the line: applications are read in the order they arrive, in a weekly review, and places are claimed in that order. One published exception — and it changes no one's place in line: every founding inspector receives one Golden Ticket, which gives one named colleague the founding rate, claimed through the same application as everyone else, and only while places remain. A ticket creates no place outside the 25: a colleague who claims through one counts against the 25 like any other member. That is the whole list of exceptions.

Who the pilot is not yet for

Plainly, so nobody joins under a wrong picture:

  • If your software is not Spectora or ISN, your move runs on an export route or an individual assessment — some things may not move, and anything that cannot is named for you at review. If your case needs assessment, we will tell you before you commit to anything.
  • If you are counting on Spex in your inbox, that is not this pilot. The pilot is Spex Live in the field — notes, proposed findings, report preparation. Reading and answering email, and Instant Reply, are not shipped in the pilot and are not promised here.
  • If you need a finished legal file before you start, our Terms document is still being drafted and the Privacy page still awaits its legal review. Both are publication gates we will not skip.
  • If you want the Concierge service defined to the last hour, its full service definition — the work, the hours, the response commitments, the exclusions — is written out in full before it can be bought. The summary on the Pricing page is the shape of it, not the contract.
