Who we are

Inspyx is made by INSPYX LLC, a Florida company based in Miami Gardens, Florida.

The company was founded in 2026 by Moises Avila, a licensed Florida home inspector.

Our founder

For years, my office was the cab of a truck. More truck drivers are killed on the job than workers in any other occupation in America, year after year (Bureau of Labor Statistics). Every mile reminded me that I needed a way out.

Home inspection was that way out. I’d always been drawn to it, and the more I learned, the more it felt like the work I was meant to do. So I did it properly: months of study, my Florida home inspector license, more training after that, and every tool the trade asks for.

Then I froze. Not at the houses, but at everything around them. I was new, and the responsibility felt heavy: a family’s biggest purchase rests on what an inspector notices and how clearly it is written down. The software made it heavier still. The tools inspectors are expected to use cost more than a new inspector can justify, and they take weeks to learn. So I parked my license and kept driving.

That is why I built Inspyx, first for myself: an app you talk to while you walk the house. You say what you see, and Spex takes the photo, marks it and files the finding in the right room, so the report is written as you go. It is what finally got me inspecting. Today I inspect part-time, on my own terms, with the tool I wish I’d had on day one.

It is also the tool I would hand any new inspector who feels nervous about starting. Spex helps you say it right: it turns what you speak into clear, professional report wording that you can read and edit, and it keeps every finding in order, so your attention stays on the house. Your judgment makes the call. Spex helps it read the way it should.

Now I want to put Inspyx in the hands of every inspector standing where I stood: licensed, equipped, ready, and held back by the very tools that were supposed to help.

What we make

Inspyx is an iPhone app for home inspectors, with a voice companion.

  • Spex is the companion you talk to during an inspection. You say what you see, and Spex takes the photo, marks it and files the finding in the right room. Every finding goes straight into the report.
  • Mia is a voice receptionist. She answers the phone, answers the usual questions and books the job on your calendar.
  • Spex Business handles the requests that come in by email or through the form on your website.

Where we are today

Inspyx is getting ready for a pilot with its first 10 inspectors. Spex works today. Mia and Spex Business arrive with the pilot.
