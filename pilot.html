The pilot, stated plainly

A pilot is a trade, and both sides of it belong on the page before you apply. Inspyx is new. The pilot is where it gets proven — on real inspections, by the first inspectors in. We would rather say that plainly than dress it up.

What pilot inspectors get

  • The founding price, locked. $69 a month for the first 25 accounts — theirs permanently.
  • Thirty days free to start. The app and Spex Live, on your real inspections.
  • Your software moved for you. Free onboarding for the first 25 — we bring across everything your current software lets us reach, done-for-you included. Anything that can't move is named for you at review, never dropped silently.
  • The guarantee, in writing. Less time to a finished report, measured against your own starting numbers. If it doesn't happen on a business we onboarded: six months free, or walk away. If you've paid and don't like it, your money back.
  • A direct line to the founder. Your feedback goes to the person who built Inspyx — not a ticket queue.

What we ask of you

  • Real inspections. Run your actual work on Inspyx — that is what the pilot is for.
  • Honest feedback. Tell us what works, what doesn't, and what is missing — plainly, as it happens.
  • An honest testimonial, if we have earned one. Your words, good or bad, when you have something to say. We won't write it for you.

The charter

The Founding 25

The first twenty-five inspector businesses in are a named group — the Founding 25 — listed on this charter, and never added to after the twenty-fifth place is claimed. Membership is not a tier and it buys no features. It is recognition, and a rate, and a say:

  • The founding rate, locked. $69 a month, permanently — held under your numbered Founding Rate Certificate, above.
  • A direct line to the founder. Your feedback goes to the person who built Inspyx — not a ticket queue.
  • An advisory say. First sight of what is being built, and a standing voice in the order it ships.
  • Your number is yours. Your certificate number appears on your certificate and in your Inspyx account, for as long as you are a member.
  • One Golden Ticket. Each founding member receives one Golden Ticket: it gives one named fellow inspector the founding rate, claimed through the same application as everyone else, while places remain. A ticket creates no place outside the 25 — a colleague who claims through one counts against the 25 like any other member — and it moves nobody up or down the line. One ticket per member. It is not a discount, and it cannot be sold.

The member list itself — names and companies, only of members who agree in writing to be listed — is published on this charter as the cohort fills. While it is short, that is the point.

Who the pilot is not yet for

Plainly, so nobody joins under a wrong picture:

  • If your software is not Spectora or ISN, your move runs on an export route or an individual assessment — some things may not move, and anything that cannot is named for you at review. If your case needs assessment, we will tell you before you commit to anything.
  • If you are counting on Spex in your inbox, that is not this pilot. The pilot is Spex Live in the field — notes, proposed findings, report preparation. Reading and answering email, and Instant Reply, are not shipped in the pilot and are not promised here.
  • If you need a finished legal file before you start, our Terms document is still being drafted and the Privacy page still awaits its legal review. Both are publication gates we will not skip.
  • If you want the Concierge service defined to the last hour, its full service definition — the work, the hours, the response commitments, the exclusions — is written out in full before it can be bought. The summary on the Pricing page is the shape of it, not the contract.
