Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

Inspyx runs on your business information, so this page matters. Here is what we hold, how we use it, and the promises that don't change.

We never sell your data

We never sell inspector data. Not your client list, not your inspection history, not your business numbers — not to anyone, not for any price. Your data is not a product we offer, and it never will be.

We never market third-party products to your clients

Your clients see your business, not somebody else's offers. We don't put third-party products or services in front of them — not in your client portal, not in your reports, not anywhere. If your clients hear from Inspyx, it's about their inspection with you.

What information Inspyx holds

What you put in: your account details, your inspections, your photos, your findings and reports, and the client details you work with. What the product keeps as you work: the record of what was captured and approved on each job.

If we move you in from your current software, we hold what moves — your profile, services, templates, contacts, and inspections — brought across under your permission, for the move and for your account after it.

How we use it

Only to run your business on Inspyx: building your reports, and — when you use Spex in the field — turning what you say into findings you approve, and keeping the record of your work straight. We don't use your data to advertise to anyone, and your information isn't used to train AI models.

Who we share it with

Only the services that keep Inspyx running — hosting, email delivery, the tools behind the features you use — and only the pieces each one needs to do its job. They hold it to run the service, never to market with it, and never to sell it. Beyond that, we share nothing unless the law requires it.

Your choices

Your account and its history stay yours. You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or close your account and take your records with you. If you ever leave, your clients and your history leave with you — the same promise as the Your Data Stays Yours page, kept here too.

Contact

Questions about any of this: support@inspyx.com. A person reads it.
