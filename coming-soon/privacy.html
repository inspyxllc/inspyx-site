Who we are

Inspyx is made by INSPYX LLC, Miami Gardens, Florida. Questions about privacy: support@inspyx.com or (888) 772-2919.

Who this covers

  • Home inspectors who use the Inspyx app or our website.
  • The people in their inspections (clients, real estate agents and others), whose details an inspector enters.

The inspector decides what to record about their clients and agents, and how to use it. Inspyx handles that information for the inspector, to provide the service. Inspectors own their client relationships, and we never sell anyone's data.

What we collect, and why

  1. Your account. Your name, email address and sign-in, handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase. We never see your password. You can add business details (your license number, logo, photo and signature), which print on your reports.
  2. Your inspections. Property addresses and details; your clients' and agents' names, phone numbers and emails; findings, notes, answers and photos (photos can show the inside of a home); your client's signature on your agreement; and payments you record. These are saved on your iPhone. They leave it when you share or export them, or when a feature below sends what it needs to do its job.
  3. Spex, the voice companion. While Spex is on, what you say, and any photo you show Spex, goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, so Spex can understand and answer. Our server keeps a usage record for each session (your account, how long it ran, and what it cost us), not the audio or the words. The captions stay on your phone. Spex listens only while it's on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  4. Property lookups. When you choose an address, our server sends it to public-record, weather and map services to fill in the inspection: RentCast (property facts), the U.S. Census Bureau's address service, the National Weather Service, NOAA and the Aviation Weather Center (the weather when you start), and Google (Street View and the view from above). Address suggestions while you type come from Apple Maps, through your iPhone.
  5. Crash reports. If the app breaks, a report of where and why goes to Sentry, our crash-reporting service. Names and inspection content are left out. You can turn this off: ☰ → Settings → Send crash reports.
  6. Your plan. Billing runs through Stripe. Stripe receives your email address and card; we never see or store card numbers. We keep your plan's status, dates and price.
  7. Your numbers. Inspyx works out how long each inspection took (time on site, time to the report) on your iPhone. Nothing leaves your phone unless you export it.
  8. Our website and phone line. If you apply for the pilot on our website (the form is a Google form) or call our phone line, we keep your name, contact details and what you tell us, so we can answer you. If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email address, the two answers you tap, and which page and which ad or link brought you, so we can write to you when the pilot opens. Our phone line is answered by Mia, an AI receptionist, through a service called Retell. Our website uses no cookies and no ad trackers.

What we never do

  • Sell anyone's data.
  • Use your clients' details to market to them.
  • Use your inspection data to advertise.

Who we share with

  • The service providers named above, each only for its part.
  • People you choose to send things to, such as a report you share.
  • Anyone the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it, or delete the app.
  • On our servers: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your account and your records from our servers right away. We keep only what the law or our accounts require: Stripe's billing records, a record of any refund, and Spex usage records.

Your choices

  • Turn crash reports off: ☰ → Settings → Send crash reports.
  • Turn Spex off at any time.
  • Export everything: ☰ → Export.
  • Delete your account in the app: ☰ → Account → Delete your account.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information: support@inspyx.com.

If you're an inspector's client or agent, ask the inspector first; we'll help them answer.

Security

  • Data on your iPhone is protected by the iPhone's own encryption.
  • Connections to our servers are encrypted.
  • Every change to an inspection is kept in a tamper-evident record, and a signed agreement keeps the exact text that was signed.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.

Children

Inspyx is for professionals, and it isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post changes on this page, and tell you in the app when a change matters.
