The guarantee, with its conditions in the open

The promise: less time to a finished report — measured against your own starting numbers, written down with you when you start.

If it doesn't happen on an inspection business Inspyx onboarded: you choose — six months free, or walk away. We can only guarantee a setup we did and verified ourselves; self-serve setup isn't covered.

If you've paid and you don't like it, you get your money back. The money-back guarantee stands, in every phase.

What it is not: not a promise of a specific number of minutes or a specific percentage. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial — the one free period; nothing stacks on it.

How the measurement works — a worked example, figures illustrative

Say your starting number, written down with you on day one, is 3 hours and 10 minutes a report. The clock starts when an inspection ends and stops when the finished report is ready to send. Your trial reports are timed the same way. If the time has not come down, the remedy above applies — six months free, or walk away.
