What your plan includes

  • The Inspyx app for iPhone.
  • Spex, the voice companion that takes your notes, photos and findings while you inspect.
  • Reports you send to your clients from your phone.
  • Your templates, agreements, clients and agents, kept in one place.
  • Help by email and phone.

30 days free

  • Your plan starts with 30 days free, once per inspector.
  • Your card is saved when you start. Your first charge comes on the day the free month ends, and the app shows that date.
  • We email you a reminder 7 days before the free month ends.
  • Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

How billing works

  • You start your plan in the Inspyx app: ☰ → Settings → Your plan.
  • Stripe, our payment processor, takes your card on its secure checkout page. That's where you choose monthly or yearly. Inspyx never sees or stores your card number.
  • Your plan renews automatically every month or every year until you cancel.
  • You get an emailed receipt for every payment.
  • Prices are in US dollars, and don't include sales tax where it applies.

Cancel anytime

  • In the app: ☰ → Settings → Your plan → Manage billing, then Cancel subscription.
  • Your plan stays on until the end of the month or year you've paid for, then it stops. Nothing more is charged.

The details are in our cancellation and refund policy.
