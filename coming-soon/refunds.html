Cancellation and refunds

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Cancel anytime

You can cancel your Inspyx plan at any time:

  • In the app: ☰ → Settings → Your plan → Manage billing, then Cancel subscription.
  • Or write to support@inspyx.com, or call (888) 772-2919, and we'll cancel it for you.

What happens when you cancel

  • In your free month: your plan ends when the free month does, and you're never charged.
  • After that: your plan stays on until the end of the month or year you've already paid for, then it stops. Nothing more is charged.
  • Changed your mind? Until that day, you can undo the cancellation in Manage billing.
  • Your inspections stay on your iPhone. Canceling doesn't remove them, and you can export them anytime: ☰ → Export.
  • Coming back: you can start a plan again whenever you like. The free month is once per inspector, and the founding price lasts only while a plan continues, so a new plan starts at the price we offer then.

Refunds

When you cancel, we don't refund the rest of a month or year you've already paid for. These are the exceptions:

  • Deleting your account. If you delete your Inspyx account (☰ → Account → Delete your account), your plan ends at once, and we refund the unused whole days of the period you've paid for. The day you delete counts as used. There's nothing to refund in the free month, since nothing was charged.
  • Charges made by mistake. If you're charged twice, or charged after you canceled, we refund that charge in full.
  • Where the law requires a refund.

Refunds go back to the card that paid. Your bank usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days.

If a payment doesn't go through

  • You'll get an email, and your card is tried again several times over the next two weeks.
  • You can update your card anytime: ☰ → Settings → Your plan → Update card.
  • If the payment still hasn't gone through after two weeks, your plan is canceled. You can start it again anytime.

Questions about a charge

Please talk to us before disputing a charge with your bank. Write to support@inspyx.com or call (888) 772-2919, and we'll make it right.
