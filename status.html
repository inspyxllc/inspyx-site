Your next actions

1. Choose your onboarding-window preference. When your place comes up, we book the onboarding call inside the window you pick. It is a preference, not an appointment — we confirm the actual time with you personally.

2. Hold your seat — $69

The deposit is one founding month, paid early: $69. It is refundable in full, any time before your onboarding — ask, and it comes back; no retention flow, no argument. If you stay, it is credited in full against your first paid month, after your 30 free days. The 30 days themselves start free either way; this holds the place, nothing else.

When your seat is held, your numbered Founding Rate Certificate is issued — the durable record of your $69 rate — and your state here becomes “seat held.”

Specimen control — the payment step itself is built with the application store at Step 7. The refund is a real commitment, stated here because the terms belong next to the button, not in a footnote.

After onboarding, one more thing is yours: a single Golden Ticket — it gives one named fellow inspector the founding rate, through the same application as everyone else, while places remain. It creates no place outside the 10 — they count against the 10 like any other member — and it moves nobody up or down the line. It arrives when your move-in is complete.
