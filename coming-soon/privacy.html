Who we are

Inspyx is made by INSPYX LLC, Miami Gardens, Florida. Questions about privacy: support@inspyx.com or (888) 772-2919.

Who this covers

  • Home inspectors who use the Inspyx app or our website.
  • The people in their inspections (clients, real estate agents and others), whose details an inspector enters or a client enters on an inspector's booking page.
  • Clients who sign an agreement, pay or open a report through Inspyx, agents who get emails about an inspection, and people who call an inspector's Mia line.
  • Visitors to inspyx.com and callers to our own phone line.

The inspector decides what to record about their clients and agents, and how to use it. Inspyx handles that information for the inspector, to provide the service. Inspectors own their client relationships, and we never sell anyone's data.

What we collect, and why

  1. Your account. Your name, email address and sign-in, handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they tell us your name and email address. We never see your password, and Face ID stays on your iPhone. If you sign in with Apple, we keep the token Apple gives us, only so that deleting your account also ends Inspyx's access at Apple. We note when you agreed to our Terms and this policy, which wording you were shown, and how you first signed in. You can add business details (your license and association numbers, a second inspector's name, your company's phone and email, logo, photo and signature), which print on your reports. Your company details, picture, logo, signature, agreements, templates and price list are kept on Inspyx's servers.
  2. Your inspections. Property addresses and details; your clients' and agents' names, phone numbers and emails; findings, notes, answers and photos (photos can show the inside of a home); your client's signature on your agreement; and your fee for the inspection, whether it's paid, and payments you record. These are saved on your iPhone and kept on our servers too, so you can see and finish them at inspyx.com, or on another iPhone or iPad signed in to your account. Our servers also keep each earlier version of what you change, so a change can be traced. Your iPhone also keeps a private copy of all of it, photo and video files included, in your account's own folder in our file store (at Supabase), so a lost or replaced iPhone never means a lost inspection: sign in on the new one and bring them back. The same folder brings an inspection's photo and video files to your other iPhone or iPad, so both show the same inspection. Nothing in it goes to anyone else's account or device. Copies go over Wi-Fi, or over mobile data only if you allow it. Photo and video files otherwise leave your iPhone only when you send a report, share or export them, or when a feature below sends what it needs to do its job.
  3. Your inspections on the website. So you can see your inspections, calendar and the people on them on inspyx.com, their details are kept on Inspyx's servers with your account.
  4. People you keep. The clients and agents you keep in People (their names, phone numbers, emails, companies and your notes about them) are kept on Inspyx's servers with your account, so they show on the website as well as in the app, and they're removed when you delete your account.
  5. Inspections from the app you used before. When you bring in your past inspections, we keep each one's date, address, client and the client's email and phone, agents, fee, services, inspector, order number and notes, and where it came from, on Inspyx's servers with your account, so they show on the website as well as in the app. They're removed when you delete your account.
  6. Bringing your data in. When you bring in a file of your clients, agents or past inspections, your iPhone or your browser reads it there: the file itself is never sent to us, only the people and inspections you choose to keep. When you convert a template, its words go through our server to Google's Gemini service (see Spex's writing help below), and on the website the converted template is kept with your account. If you email us your old templates to move for you, we use them only to do that. To bring your data in straight from your old app, you paste your own key: a Spectora API key, or an ISN access key and secret with the address of your ISN page. In the app, your iPhone asks Spectora or ISN directly. On the website, our server asks them for each page and passes it to your browser without keeping it, and it only ever contacts Spectora's and ISN's own addresses. Your key is used only for those requests: we never keep it or write it down. If ISN's answer includes other users' keys, we drop them at once. So no one can use our website to flood Spectora or ISN, our server counts how many requests your account makes each hour, never what they carry, and the count is removed when you delete your account.
  7. Your booking page. If you turn on your booking page, your clients can book you at inspyx.com/book/your-name. They see your name, company, services, prices and open times. We work out your open times from the hours you set and your inspections' times only (when each starts and ends, never the address or the client). When a client books, we keep what they enter (their name, email and phone, their agent's name, email and phone, the property's address and details, the services they pick, any discount code, and their notes) and send it to your app. If they stop partway, we keep what they entered and show it to you, so you can follow up.
  8. Automatic emails. Unless you turn them off, Inspyx emails your client (and, where you choose, their agent and the listing agent) when they book or the time moves, to remind them to sign or pay, the day before the inspection, if a booking is cancelled, and, after your client opens their report, to ask for a Google review. These emails, and booking and report emails, go out through Amazon's email service. We keep each email we send (who it went to, its subject and its words) and note whether it was delivered, bounced or marked as spam, so you can see it reached them. We don't track whether an email is opened.
  9. Agreements signed online. When a client books, we email them your agreement to sign on their phone or computer. We keep the exact text they were sent, when they opened it, their initials and drawn signature, their typed name, when they signed, their device's browser name, and a scrambled form of their internet address, so the signature can be shown to be theirs. The signed copy (a PDF) is emailed to them and to you, and kept on our servers while your account is open. We remind a client to sign only while they haven't, at most three times, and never after the inspection starts.
  10. Reports you send. When you send a report, your client gets a private link to it at inspyx.com. We keep the report there, with its photos, videos and PDF, while your account is open, and note when each link is first and last opened, and how often, so you can see your client has it. On their report link, your client or their agent can list the repairs or credits they want for its findings. We keep their list with the report, and any copy they choose to share at inspyx.com/repair. They send it themselves; Inspyx sends nothing.
  11. Card payments from your clients. If you take card payments, your client pays on Stripe's page, into your own Stripe account. Stripe receives their card; we never see it. We keep what was paid, when, for which inspection, the email it was sent to, any card fee you add, and Stripe's receipt link, so your app and the website can show it.
  12. Texts to your clients. Only to a client who agreed to texts, and only about their own inspection. Texts go out through Amazon's text service, from Inspyx's number on your behalf. We keep each text and when it was sent. If someone replies STOP, we keep their number, with no name or account, so they're never texted again.
  13. Mia, your phone line. If you turn on Mia for your business, calls to your Mia number are answered by Mia through Retell, our phone service, which turns the call into words so Mia can answer. We keep each call's time and length (for your minutes), the number that called, whether Mia put the call through to you, and the message Mia takes: the caller's name and number, why they called, any address, services or times they mention, what Mia told them it would cost, and a short summary of the call. Mia emails that message to you, and it shows in your app. Mia tells every caller the call may be recorded: Retell records the call and keeps the recording and a word-for-word transcript in its own system. We don't copy either to our own servers.
  14. Emails you forward. If you forward a client's email to your Inspyx address (your-name@book.inspyx.com), we keep its sender, subject and words for 90 days so Spex can draft your reply. Attachments aren't kept. Amazon holds the email itself for at most 7 days, and we delete it sooner once we've kept its words. Spex only drafts: nothing is sent until you tap Send.
  15. Spex, the voice companion. While Spex is on, what you say, what your camera shows Spex, and this inspection's rooms, items and findings go through our server to Google's Gemini service, so Spex can understand and answer. Our server keeps a usage record for each session (your account, the inspection, how long it ran, how much it used, and what it cost us), not the audio or the words. The captions stay on your phone, and what you say for a finding is kept with that finding on your iPhone. Spex listens only while it's on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  16. Spex Chat and Spex's writing help. When you type to Spex, or ask it to rewrite a finding (in the app or on the website), an email or a text, draft a reply, check a label's details, or convert a template, what you send goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, which writes the answer. For Spex Chat, that includes the photos, videos or documents you attach, and the records Spex reads to answer you: your profile, your inspections and the people on them (names, phone numbers and emails), payments, People, prices, templates, agreements, and emails you forwarded. Nothing changes until you keep it. Your chats stay on your iPhone. Our server keeps a usage record (your account, how many messages, and what it cost us), not your words.
  17. Property lookups. When you choose an address, our server uses it to fill in the inspection: it sends the address to RentCast (property facts) and the U.S. Census Bureau's address service, and the home's map position to Google (the roof from above), the U.S. Geological Survey (the ground's height), and the National Weather Service, NOAA and the Aviation Weather Center (the weather when you start). We keep what was found for 90 days under a scrambled form of the address, so the same house isn't looked up twice. Address suggestions while you type, and the 3D view of the house, come from Apple Maps, through your iPhone.
  18. Crash reports. If the app breaks or freezes, a report of where and why goes to Inspyx's own server, hosted by Supabase, with your iPhone's model and iOS version, and when the app was last opened or put away. The app also sends a short note when something goes wrong without breaking it, such as a booking that couldn't be saved. We leave out your name, and remove email addresses and phone numbers. We keep each report for up to 90 days. You can turn this off: More → Settings → Send crash reports.
  19. Your plan. Billing runs through Stripe. Stripe receives your email address and card; we never see or store card numbers. We keep your plan's status, dates and price.
  20. Your numbers. Inspyx works out how long each inspection took (time on site, time to the report) on your iPhone. If you tick "Be a founding inspector" when you sign up, your app sends Inspyx these numbers for each inspection you finish in your first month: the inspection's number in Inspyx, when it started, when you were last on site, when the report was made and shared, and how many findings (and how many Spex added), photos and items it has. Never a client's name, an address, or anything your report says. After your first month, nothing more is kept. If you don't tick the box, nothing leaves your phone unless you export it.
  21. Try Spex on our website. If you try Spex on inspyx.com, what you say goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, so Spex can answer. We keep no recording and no words. To keep the demo fair, our server keeps a scrambled form of your internet address for that day only, made new each day so one day's can't be matched with another's, and deletes it within two days.
  22. Our website and phone line. If you apply for the pilot by calling our phone line, Mia takes your name, company, email and phone, the software you use now, your state, how many inspections you do, and your answers, and we keep them in our records and in a Google form, so we can answer you. If you call us about anything else, we keep your name, contact details and what you tell us. If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email address, the two answers you tap, and which page and which ad or link brought you, so we can write to you when the pilot opens. Our phone line is answered by Mia, an AI receptionist, through a service called Retell, which records our calls and keeps the recordings and transcripts; Mia tells every caller the call may be recorded. Our website uses no cookies and no ad trackers. If you sign in on the website, your browser keeps your sign-in in its own storage until you sign out.

How we use it

  • To run Inspyx for you: your inspections, reports, bookings, agreements, payments, texts, emails, Mia and Spex.
  • To help you when you ask us, and to keep accounts safe and stop abuse.
  • To make Inspyx better: from crash reports; from patterns across many inspectors; and, unless you turn it off, from your findings: what Spex wrote and what you kept or changed it to, with your clients' and agents' names and contact details and the property's address taken out. We use them to test Spex and teach it to be more accurate. Photos are used only if you turn on “Include photos”, because they can show the inside of a home. We never read one inspector's records for their own sake, never sell this, and never give it to another company to train its own AI.
  • To tell you about Inspyx: emails about your account and plan, which you always get, and news about Inspyx, which you can stop with the link in any of them.
  • To show what Inspyx does: we may publish totals and averages from founders' numbers (for example, "founders finished their reports 40% faster"), never naming you or your clients.
  • Testimonials: we use your name, company, picture or words in our marketing only if you say yes, and we stop if you change your mind.

What we never do

  • Sell anyone's data.
  • Use your clients' details to market to them.
  • Use your inspection data to advertise.
  • Let our AI provider train its models on what you send: Google handles it for us under terms that don't allow that.
  • Sell anyone's mobile number or their OK to receive texts, or share either with third parties for marketing. A client's number and their text consent are used only to send texts about their own inspection, from Inspyx's number on their inspector's behalf. When someone agrees to texts on a booking page, we keep the words they agreed to, when, and a scrambled form of their internet address, as proof of their consent. Replying STOP ends the texts.

Who we share with

  • Our service providers, each only for its part: Supabase (our database, file store and sign-in), Google Cloud (Spex and Gemini, Spex's usage records, and the roof from above), Amazon Web Services (emails and texts), Stripe (your plan, and your clients' card payments), Retell (Mia's calls), Vercel (our website), Apple (sign-in with Apple, address suggestions and the 3D view), Google (sign-in with Google), RentCast and the public address, map and weather services named above, and Google Forms (pilot applications).
  • People you choose to send things to, such as a report you share.
  • Real estate agents you email a report to. When you email a report to someone as their agent, Inspyx gives that agent a private link to one page listing the reports you have emailed to that same address: each property's address and date, and the report. We keep the agent's email address for it, and note when the page is opened. It shows only what you already sent them, and you can turn it off at any time. To stop anyone guessing an agent page's private link, our server keeps a scrambled form of the visitor's internet address, made new each day, and deletes it within two days.
  • A buyer of the business: if INSPYX LLC is ever merged or sold, your information moves with it, and this policy still applies to it.
  • Anyone the law requires us to.

Where it's kept

Inspyx's servers, and our providers' for the parts they handle, are in the United States. Spex Chat and Spex's writing help use Google's worldwide Gemini service, which may answer from outside the United States.

How long we keep it

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it, delete your account, or delete the app.
  • On our servers: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your account and your records from our servers right away. We keep only what the law or our accounts require: Stripe's billing records, a record of any refund, and Spex usage records; and findings used to improve Spex, which name no one (see below). If Apple won't yet take back a sign-in token, we keep that token, naming no account, only until Apple does. If you get your money back, we keep a scrambled form of your email address and card even after your account is deleted, only so the same person can't get a second refund. Numbers that replied STOP stay on our do-not-text list, with no name or account. Our providers keep what they handle under their own terms.
  • Booking pages: a booking someone started but didn't finish, 30 days; finished bookings and signed agreements, while your account is open; forwarded emails, 90 days. To stop anyone scraping a booking page, our server keeps a scrambled form of the visitor's internet address, made new each day, and deletes it within two days.
  • Emails and texts we send for you, Mia's calls and messages, your numbers, and reports you send: while your account is open. Totals and averages already worked out from founders' numbers name no one, so they stay.
  • Cloud copies of your iPhone: while your account is open. Deleting your account deletes them.
  • Pilot applications made by phone: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days. They name no one, so deleting your account doesn't change that.
  • Findings used to improve Spex: with names, contact details and addresses taken out, they name no one, so they stay after you delete your account, like the totals from founders' numbers. Photos you allowed are removed when you delete your account. Ask support@inspyx.com and we'll remove the rest of yours too.

Your choices

  • Turn crash reports off: More → Settings → Send crash reports.
  • Let cloud copies use mobile data, or keep them to Wi-Fi: More → Settings → Use mobile data for photos and clips.
  • Turn Spex off at any time.
  • Help improve Spex: on unless you turn it off, More → Settings → Help improve Spex. “Include photos” is off unless you turn it on. Turning either off stops any new use from then on.
  • Export your inspections, reports, photos, templates, agreements and profile: More → Export.
  • Delete your account in the app: More → Account → Delete your account.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information: support@inspyx.com.
  • Stop Inspyx's news emails with the link in any of them.
  • Take back a yes to a testimonial: write to support@inspyx.com.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what information we hold about you, for a copy of it, to correct it, or to delete it, and you won't be treated differently for asking. Write to support@inspyx.com. We answer within 45 days, and we may need to confirm it's you first. We don't sell personal information, or share it for advertising that follows you around the web.

Security

  • Data on your iPhone is protected by the iPhone's own encryption.
  • Connections to our servers are encrypted.
  • On your iPhone, every change to an inspection is kept in a tamper-evident record, and a signed agreement keeps the exact text that was signed.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.

Children

Inspyx is for professionals, and it isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post changes on this page, and tell you in the app when a change matters.
