Who we are

Inspyx is made by INSPYX LLC, Miami Gardens, Florida. Questions about privacy: support@inspyx.com or (888) 772-2919.

Who this covers

  • Home inspectors who use the Inspyx app or our website.
  • The people in their inspections (clients, real estate agents and others), whose details an inspector enters or a client enters on an inspector's booking page.

The inspector decides what to record about their clients and agents, and how to use it. Inspyx handles that information for the inspector, to provide the service. Inspectors own their client relationships, and we never sell anyone's data.

What we collect, and why

  1. Your account. Your name, email address and sign-in, handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase. We never see your password. You can add business details (your license number, logo, photo and signature), which print on your reports. Your company details, picture, logo, signature and agreements are kept on Inspyx's servers.
  2. Your inspections. Property addresses and details; your clients' and agents' names, phone numbers and emails; findings, notes, answers and photos (photos can show the inside of a home); your client's signature on your agreement; and payments you record. These are saved on your iPhone. They leave it when you share or export them, or when a feature below sends what it needs to do its job.
  3. Your booking page. If you turn on your booking page, your clients can book you at inspyx.com/book/your-name. They see your name, company, services, prices and open times. We work out your open times from the hours you set and your inspections' times only (when each starts and ends, never the address or the client). When a client books, we keep what they enter (their name, email, phone, the property's address and details, and the services they pick) and send it to your app. Booking emails (and Inspyx's other automatic emails) go out through Amazon's email service. If they stop partway, we keep what they entered for 30 days so you can follow up.
  4. Agreements signed online. When a client books, we email them your agreement to sign on their phone or computer. We keep the exact text they were sent, their initials and drawn signature, their typed name, when they signed, their device's browser name, and a scrambled form of their internet address, so the signature can be shown to be theirs. The signed copy (a PDF) is emailed to them and to you, and kept on our servers while your account is open. We remind a client to sign only while they haven't, at most three times, and never after the inspection starts.
  5. Emails you forward. If you forward a client's email to your Inspyx address (your-name@book.inspyx.com), we keep its sender, subject and words for 90 days so Spex can draft your reply. Attachments aren't kept. Amazon holds the email itself only until we've kept its words, and never longer than 7 days. Spex only drafts: nothing is sent until you tap Send.
  6. Spex, the voice companion. While Spex is on, what you say, and any photo you show Spex, goes through our server to Google's Gemini service, so Spex can understand and answer. Our server keeps a usage record for each session (your account, how long it ran, and what it cost us), not the audio or the words. The captions stay on your phone. Spex listens only while it's on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  7. Property lookups. When you choose an address, our server sends it to public-record, weather and map services to fill in the inspection: RentCast (property facts), the U.S. Census Bureau's address service, the National Weather Service, NOAA and the Aviation Weather Center (the weather when you start), and Google (Street View and the view from above). Address suggestions while you type come from Apple Maps, through your iPhone.
  8. Crash reports. If the app breaks, a report of where and why goes to Sentry, our crash-reporting service. Names and inspection content are left out. You can turn this off: ☰ → Settings → Send crash reports.
  9. Your plan. Billing runs through Stripe. Stripe receives your email address and card; we never see or store card numbers. We keep your plan's status, dates and price.
  10. Your numbers. Inspyx works out how long each inspection took (time on site, time to the report) on your iPhone. Nothing leaves your phone unless you export it.
  11. Our website and phone line. If you apply for the pilot on our website (the form is a Google form) or call our phone line, we keep your name, contact details and what you tell us, so we can answer you. If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email address, the two answers you tap, and which page and which ad or link brought you, so we can write to you when the pilot opens. Our phone line is answered by Mia, an AI receptionist, through a service called Retell. Our website uses no cookies and no ad trackers.

What we never do

  • Sell anyone's data.
  • Use your clients' details to market to them.
  • Use your inspection data to advertise.
  • Sell anyone's mobile number or their OK to receive texts, or share either with third parties for marketing. A client's number and their text consent are used only to send texts about their own inspection, from Inspyx's number on their inspector's behalf. When someone agrees to texts on a booking page, we keep the words they agreed to, when, and a scrambled form of their internet address, as proof of their consent. Replying STOP ends the texts.

Who we share with

  • The service providers named above, each only for its part.
  • People you choose to send things to, such as a report you share.
  • Real estate agents you email a report to. When you email a report to someone as their agent, Inspyx gives that agent a private link to one page listing the reports you have emailed to that same address: each property's address and date, and the report. It shows only what you already sent them, and you can turn it off at any time. To stop anyone guessing an agent page's private link, our server keeps a scrambled form of the visitor's internet address for up to a day, then deletes it.
  • Anyone the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it, delete your account, or delete the app.
  • On our servers: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your account and your records from our servers right away. We keep only what the law or our accounts require: Stripe's billing records, a record of any refund, and Spex usage records. If you get your money back, we keep a scrambled form of your email address and card even after your account is deleted, only so the same person can't get a second refund.
  • Booking pages: a booking someone started but didn't finish, 30 days; finished bookings and signed agreements, while your account is open; forwarded emails, 90 days. To stop anyone scraping a booking page, our server keeps a scrambled form of the visitor's internet address for up to a day.

Your choices

  • Turn crash reports off: ☰ → Settings → Send crash reports.
  • Turn Spex off at any time.
  • Export everything: ☰ → Export.
  • Delete your account in the app: ☰ → Account → Delete your account.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information: support@inspyx.com.

If you're an inspector's client or agent, ask the inspector first; we'll help them answer.

Security

  • Data on your iPhone is protected by the iPhone's own encryption.
  • Connections to our servers are encrypted.
  • Every change to an inspection is kept in a tamper-evident record, and a signed agreement keeps the exact text that was signed.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.

Children

Inspyx is for professionals, and it isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post changes on this page, and tell you in the app when a change matters.
