Contact us

  • Email: support@inspyx.com
  • Phone: (888) 772-2919. Mia, our voice receptionist, answers. She can take a message or connect you.

We answer every message, usually within one business day.

Your plan and billing

  • See or change your plan in the app: ☰ → Settings → Your plan.
  • Update your card: ☰ → Settings → Your plan → Update card.
  • Cancel: ☰ → Settings → Your plan → Manage billing, then Cancel subscription. Or ask us, and we'll cancel it for you.

What happens when you cancel, and when we refund, is in our cancellation and refund policy. Prices are on the pricing page.

Your account

  • Forgot your password? Contact us and we will start a reset for you.
  • Delete your account: in the app, ☰ → Account → Delete your account.

Your data

Your inspections stay on your iPhone, and you can export them anytime: ☰ → Export.

What we collect and what we never do is in our privacy policy. The rules for using Inspyx are in our terms.

Report a problem

Write to support@inspyx.com and tell us:

  • what you were doing when it happened,
  • what you expected, and what happened instead,
  • your iPhone model.

A screenshot or a screen recording helps us most.
